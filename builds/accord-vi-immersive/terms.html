Attending Accord VI

Accord VI is open to students aged 12 and up; roughly 20% of past attendees are accompanying teachers, parents or family. A full two-night pass is PKR 3,500; a concert-only ticket for 18 October is PKR 2,500. Submitting the registration form is an expression of interest, not a confirmed reservation — payment and ticketing logistics will be confirmed with you separately closer to the date. payment process TBD

Entry & conduct

We reserve the right to carry out reasonable security/bag checks at entry and to refuse or remove entry for unsafe, illegal, or disruptive behaviour. Please follow venue staff instructions at all times.

Changes to the event

Line-up, schedule, and module/activity details may change from what's shown on this site, including for reasons outside our control. We'll communicate material changes where we can.

Photography & video

Accord VI may be photographed or filmed for future promotional use (this site, social media, recruitment materials). By attending, you may appear in this footage. If you'd rather not be featured, let event staff know on the day.

Sponsorship

The tiers on our sponsorship page describe typical entitlements. A sponsorship isn't final until confirmed in writing between your organization and the Accord VI organizing committee.

Liability

Attend at your own risk with respect to ordinary event hazards (crowds, noise, weather for any outdoor portions). We're not liable for loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings.
