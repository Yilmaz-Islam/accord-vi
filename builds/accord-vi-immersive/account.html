    Before we create your account

    Please read the Terms and the Privacy Policy. This is the short version; the full pages are linked below.

    Terms

    • Registering is an expression of interest, not a confirmed reservation.
    • We may carry out bag checks at entry, and refuse or remove anyone who is unsafe, breaks the law or is disruptive.
    • The line-up, schedule and activities can change.
    • You may appear in photos and video of the event that we use to promote Accord VI. Tell event staff on the day if you would rather not.
    • You attend at your own risk, and we are not liable for lost, stolen or damaged belongings.

    Privacy

    • We store your name, your email, your password (scrambled, so nobody can read it), your registration, your payment receipt photo and your tickets.
    • We use it to run your account, check your payment, email you and run the event. We do not sell it, and there are no advertising trackers on this site.
    • The committee members who check payments can see your registration and receipt. Gate staff see the name on a ticket when they scan it.
    • Cloudflare and EmailJS handle your information for us, to run the site and send emails.
    • You can download your data or delete your account from your Account page. Once your payment is confirmed we keep that record, and you would need to contact us to delete it.
    • If you are under 18, we encourage you to register with a parent, guardian or teacher knowing about it.
